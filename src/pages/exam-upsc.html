UPSC age limit at a glance

You must be at least 21 and under 32 on 1 August of the exam year. Reserved categories get extra years on the upper limit: 3 for OBC, 5 for SC/ST and 10 for persons with benchmark disabilities (13 for OBC and 15 for SC/ST PwBD candidates). Ex-servicemen get 5 years and defence personnel disabled in operations 3 years, with conditions set out in the notice.

How UPSC counts your age

Age is counted on 1 August of the exam year, not on the exam date. “21 to 32” means you must have turned 21 by that day and must not yet have turned 32. The calculator above works out the exact range of birth dates for your category and every exam year you fit, assuming the rules stay the same.
