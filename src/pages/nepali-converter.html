How to convert a date

  1. Choose the direction: BS to AD (Nepali to English) or AD to BS (English to Nepali).

  3. The converted date and its weekday appear immediately. Use “Age from this date” to see someone’s exact age.

Why a converter is needed

Bikram Sambat is roughly 56 years and 8 months ahead of AD, but its months don’t have fixed lengths. Each year’s month lengths are set in advance, between 29 and 32 days, so the gap between the two calendars shifts by a few days from month to month. This converter uses the published month lengths for every year from 2000 to 2090 BS.
