How the age difference is calculated

The calculator starts from the older person’s date of birth and counts forward to the younger person’s: first whole years, then whole months, then the days left over. If Asha was born on 10 March 1990 and Rohan on 25 July 1993, Asha is older by 3 years, 4 months and 15 days.

The total in days counts every calendar day between the two dates, including 29 February in leap years.

Common uses

  • Siblings, twins born either side of midnight, or cousins: who is older and by exactly how much.
  • Couples: the age gap for a wedding speech, a card or a form.
  • Parents and children: how old a parent was when a child was born.
