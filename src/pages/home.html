How the age calculator works

The calculator counts whole years from your date of birth (DOB), then whole months, then the days left over. Someone born on 15 August 1994 is 32 years, 2 months and 24 days old on 8 November 2026.

Months are counted to the same date each month. If that date doesn’t exist in a month (the 31st in a 30-day month, or 29 February in a normal year), the last day of the month is used instead. Totals in days, weeks and hours count every calendar day between the two dates, including leap days.

Calculate age on a specific date, year, or today (exam & job cutoff eligibility)

Whether you need to check your exact age on today's date, calculate how old you will be in a future calendar year, or verify eligibility for recruitment notices with fixed cutoff dates (such as 1 January or 1 August), simply adjust the target date:

  1. Enter your date of birth (DOB).
  2. Change “Age as on” to the specific target date or calendar year given in the notification.
  3. Open “Check eligibility limit” and enter the minimum and maximum limits.

The result immediately shows whether you meet the cutoff limit and the exact range of birth dates that qualify. Birthdays on 29 February

If you were born on 29 February, this calculator treats 28 February as your birthday in years that aren’t leap years. Some countries use 1 March for legal purposes.

Your data stays on your device

Everything is calculated in your browser. Your date of birth is never sent to a server. “Copy link to this result” puts the two dates in the web address, so you can bookmark or share a result if you want to.
