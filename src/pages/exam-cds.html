CDS age limit at a glance

  • IMA and Indian Naval Academy: 19 to 24, unmarried men.
  • Air Force Academy: 20 to 24 (up to 26 with a valid Commercial Pilot Licence). Candidates under 25 must be unmarried.
  • Officers’ Training Academy (SSC): 19 to 25. Unmarried men; unmarried women, and issueless widows or divorcees who have not remarried.

There is no category-based relaxation. Each notice states the exact birth-date range for every academy.
