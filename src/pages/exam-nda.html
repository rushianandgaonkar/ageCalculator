NDA age calculator & eligibility at a glance

The NDA and Naval Academy exam is held twice a year by UPSC. Use this online NDA age calculator to test your date of birth (DOB) against the official three-year eligibility window (approximately 16½ to 19½ years at course commencement). Only unmarried candidates can apply, and the limit is uniform across all categories with no reservation relaxation.
