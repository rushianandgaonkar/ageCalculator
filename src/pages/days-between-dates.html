How date duration is measured

Counting days between two calendar points follows standard astronomical time arithmetic. Each calendar day runs from midnight to midnight UTC. By convention, date duration calculates elapsed time: the starting day is counted, and the target day marks the finish line.

When you need to know how many calendar days are in a schedule inclusive of both borders, such as hotel stays, rental periods, or legal notice windows, turn on the inclusive option.

Calendar breakdown vs total days

Total days provides the absolute count of 24-hour periods elapsed between two dates. The calendar breakdown translates that number into completed years, months and leftover days according to the Gregorian calendar.

Because months have unequal lengths (28, 29, 30 or 31 days), stepping full calendar months reflects how humans describe time. For instance, from 15 January to 15 March is exactly 2 calendar months, covering 59 days in standard years and 60 days in leap years.
