Business days vs calendar days

Calendar days count every passing 24-hour cycle regardless of the day of the week. Business days (or working days) count only the days when commerce and official services typically operate: Monday through Friday.

Because every standard 7-day week contains 5 business days and 2 weekend days, a timeframe of 10 business days spans 14 calendar days, and 20 business days spans approximately 28 calendar days (4 full calendar weeks).

Common working day benchmarks

  • 3 to 5 business days: standard domestic courier delivery and wire transfer processing.
  • 10 business days: typical government passport renewals and payroll processing cycles.
  • 20 business days: one standard month of full-time work (approximately 160 billable hours).
  • 60 business days: quarterly corporate projects and standard legal dispute response limits.

Frequently asked questions

COMMON QUESTIONS. Clear answers about this calculator and how to understand its results.

What is a business day?

A business day is an official working day of the week: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are treated as weekend days and are excluded from the count.

How do you calculate business days between two dates?

Select “Between two dates”, enter your start date and end date. The calculator counts all intervening weekdays while ignoring Saturdays and Sundays.

How do you add business days to a date?

Select “Add or subtract business days”, enter your start date, choose whether to add or subtract, and enter the number of working days. The calculator advances day by day, jumping over every weekend.

Are public holidays excluded from the business days count?

Public and bank holidays vary significantly by country, state and company policy. This tool strictly calculates standard Monday through Friday workdays. If your project observes specific national holidays, subtract those days from the total.

How are working hours calculated?

Working hours multiply the total number of business days by the daily working hours (8 hours by default). You can adjust the hours per day field to match your schedule.
