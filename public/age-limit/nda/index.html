NDA age limit at a glance

The NDA and Naval Academy exam is held twice a year. Each notice gives a three-year window of birth dates, which works out to about 16½ to 19½ years at the start of the course. Only unmarried candidates can apply, and the limit is the same for every category, with no relaxation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the age limit for NDA?

Roughly 16½ to 19½ years at the start of the course. UPSC gives the exact range as dates of birth in each notice: for NDA (II) 2026, born between 1 January 2008 and 1 January 2011, both dates included.

Which NDA can I write if I was born in 2009?

If the usual pattern holds, a 2009 birth date fits NDA (I) 2027 and NDA (II) 2027 (born 1 July 2008 to 1 July 2011 and 1 January 2009 to 1 January 2012, expected). Enter your exact date above to check.

Is there any age relaxation for SC, ST or OBC in NDA?

No. The NDA age limit is the same for every category.

Can girls apply for NDA?

Yes. Unmarried women and men can both apply, and must not marry until training is complete.

Can I apply while in Class 12?

Yes. Students appearing in Class 12 can apply, as long as they pass before the deadline given in the notice.
