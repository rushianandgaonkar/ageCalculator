CDS age limit at a glance

  • IMA and Indian Naval Academy: 19 to 24, unmarried men.
  • Air Force Academy: 20 to 24 (up to 26 with a valid Commercial Pilot Licence). Candidates under 25 must be unmarried.
  • Officers’ Training Academy (SSC): 19 to 25. Unmarried men; unmarried women, and issueless widows or divorcees who have not remarried.

There is no category-based relaxation. Each notice states the exact birth-date range for every academy.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about this calculator and how to understand its results.

What is the maximum age for CDS?

24 for IMA, the Naval Academy and the Air Force Academy, 26 for AFA candidates with a valid Commercial Pilot Licence, and 25 for OTA (Short Service Commission). The exact limits are given as dates of birth in each notice.

What were the CDS (II) 2026 dates of birth?

IMA and INA: 1 July 2003 to 1 July 2008. AFA: 1 July 2003 to 1 July 2007 (from 1 July 2001 with a CPL). OTA men and women: 1 July 2002 to 1 July 2008. All dates are included.

Can women apply through CDS?

Yes, for the Officers’ Training Academy (SSC women, non-technical): unmarried women, and issueless widows or divorcees who have not remarried. The CDS (II) 2026 notice lists IMA and INA for unmarried men only.

Is there age relaxation for SC, ST or OBC in CDS?

No. The CDS age limits are the same for every category.

Can married men apply for CDS?

Only for AFA if aged 25 or over, and they are not given married accommodation during training. IMA, INA and OTA require unmarried men.
