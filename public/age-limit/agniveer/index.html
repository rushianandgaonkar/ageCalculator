Agniveer age limit at a glance

Under the Agnipath scheme each recruitment notice gives a window of birth dates. For the Army’s 2026–27 cycle the window is 17½ to 22 years, after the upper limit was raised from 21. The Air Force sets a window for each Agniveervayu intake, and candidates must be unmarried.

Frequently asked questions

What is the age limit for Army Agniveer?

For the 2026–27 cycle, 17½ to 22 years: born between 1 July 2005 and 1 July 2009. The upper limit was raised from 21 to 22 for this cycle.

What is the age limit for Air Force Agniveervayu?

It is set for each intake. For Intake 02/2027, candidates born between 1 July 2005 and 1 January 2010 could apply; for Intake 01/2027, born between 1 January 2006 and 1 July 2009.

When does the next Agniveer recruitment open?

Army Agniveer registration usually opens in February, and the Air Force runs two Agniveervayu intakes a year. This page is updated when each new notice is published.

Can women become Agniveers?

Yes. The Air Force accepts unmarried men and women as Agniveervayu, and the Army recruits women as Agniveer (General Duty) Women Military Police.

Is the Navy covered?

Not yet. Navy Agniveer (SSR and MR) notices set their own birth-date windows; check joinindiannavy.gov.in.
