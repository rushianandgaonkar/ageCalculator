SSC age limit at a glance

  • SSC GD (Constable / Rifleman): 18 to 23 years on 1 January of the exam year.
  • SSC CGL: depends on the post, from 18–27 to 18–32 years on 1 August.
  • Relaxation: OBC 3 years, SC/ST 5 years; for CGL also PwBD 10 years (13 OBC, 15 SC/ST); for GD also 5, 8 or 10 years for children and dependents of 1984 riot victims. Ex-servicemen get 3 years after deducting military service.

Other relaxations in the CGL notice

The CGL notice also allows Central Government civilian employees with three years of continuous service up to 40 years (45 for SC/ST) for some posts, and widows or divorced women not remarried up to 35 years (40 for SC/ST) for some posts. These depend on the post, so check the notice’s post-wise table.

Frequently asked questions

What is the age limit for SSC GD 2027?

If the 2026 rules continue, 18 to 23 years on 1 January 2027 for General candidates, which means born between 2 January 2004 and 1 January 2009. OBC candidates get 3 extra years and SC/ST candidates 5. The GD 2027 notice had not been published when this page was last checked.

What was the crucial date for SSC GD 2026?

1 January 2026. General candidates had to be born between 2 January 2003 and 1 January 2008.

Which SSC CGL posts have the 18–27 age limit?

Mostly Group C posts such as Auditor, Accountant, Tax Assistant, Postal Assistant and Sorting Assistant. Inspector posts are usually 18–30, Assistant Section Officer 20–30 and Junior Statistical Officer 18–32. Each post’s limit is listed in the notice.

How does the ex-servicemen relaxation work?

Ex-servicemen get 3 years after deducting their military service from their actual age, so the result depends on your service length. Work it out with the notice; it isn’t included in the calculator.

Does SSC count age on the exam date?

No. Age is counted on the crucial date in the notice: 1 January for GD and 1 August for CGL in recent years.
