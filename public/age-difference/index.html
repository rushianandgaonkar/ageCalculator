How the age difference is calculated

The calculator starts from the older person’s date of birth and counts forward to the younger person’s: first whole years, then whole months, then the days left over. If Asha was born on 10 March 1990 and Rohan on 25 July 1993, Asha is older by 3 years, 4 months and 15 days.

The total in days counts every calendar day between the two dates, including 29 February in leap years.

Common uses

  • Siblings, twins born either side of midnight, or cousins: who is older and by exactly how much.
  • Couples: the age gap for a wedding speech, a card or a form.
  • Parents and children: how old a parent was when a child was born.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about this calculator and how to understand its results.

How do I calculate the age difference between two people?

Enter both dates of birth. The calculator counts whole years, then whole months, then the remaining days from the older person’s birthday to the younger person’s.

Does it matter which person I enter first?

No. The calculator works out who is older and shows the difference the same way either way round.

Is the age difference the same as subtracting birth years?

Not always. Two people born in 1990 and 1993 can be 2 or 3 years apart, depending on their birthdays. This calculator uses the full dates, so the result is exact.

Does the age difference ever change?

No. The gap between two birth dates is fixed. What changes is how big it feels: 5 years matters more at 10 than at 50.

Are the names or dates saved?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser. Names are never stored or added to the page address.
