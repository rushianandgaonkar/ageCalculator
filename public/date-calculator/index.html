Days from today hub

Calculating future or past deadlines from today is one of the most frequent date tasks. Common legal, financial, healthcare and travel windows include:

  • 30 days from today: standard invoice payment terms, 30-day free trials, and short notice periods.
  • 60 days from today: real estate closing periods and insurance claim filing windows.
  • 90 days from today: corporate fiscal quarters, tourist visas, and probationary employment periods.
  • 100 days from today: project milestones and traditional school hundred-day celebrations.
  • 6 months from today: passport expiration requirements and semi-annual review cycles.

How date addition and subtraction works

Adding days advances the calendar day by day, taking into account the varying lengths of each month (28, 29, 30 or 31 days) as well as leap years. When you select “Business days”, the engine steps through weekdays only, skipping Saturdays and Sundays.

When adding full calendar months or years, the calculation steps whole months forward. If the resulting month does not contain the original day number (for instance, stepping 1 month from 31 March into 30-day April), the date adjusts to the final day of that target month.

Frequently asked questions

COMMON QUESTIONS. Clear answers about this calculator and how to understand its results.

How do you add or subtract days from a date?

Enter a start date, select whether you want to add or subtract, and enter the number of days, weeks, months or years. The calculator immediately displays the exact calendar date and weekday.

How does the calculator handle months with different numbers of days?

When adding calendar months, the calculator preserves the original day of the month where possible. If the resulting month has fewer days (for example, adding 1 month to 31 January), the result clamps to the last valid day of that month (28 or 29 February).

Does this calculator count leap days?

Yes. When adding or subtracting days across February, leap years are evaluated according to the standard Gregorian calendar rules. A leap year adds 29 February as a distinct 24-hour day.

Can I calculate business days only?

Yes. Change the unit selector from “Days” to “Business days”. The calculator will skip Saturdays and Sundays, advancing or retreating only through standard workdays (Monday to Friday).

Can I find what date is 30, 60 or 90 days from today?

Yes. Click any of the quick preset buttons below the form to immediately calculate 30, 60, 90 or 100 days, or 6 months from today.
