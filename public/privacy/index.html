Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026.

Summary

The calculators on Ageason (https://ageason.com) run in your browser. Dates and other details you type into them are not sent to us and are not stored by us. We don’t ask you to create an account.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, our hosting provider receives standard technical information when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse, and is kept only as long as the provider needs it for those purposes.

Storage on your device

Some pages remember display preferences on your device, such as whether to show Arabic-Indic or Nepali digits. This is stored in your browser’s local storage, never leaves your device, and can be cleared at any time in your browser settings.

If you use “Copy link to this result”, the dates you entered are added to the page address so the link reproduces the result. Anyone you share that link with can see those dates.

Advertising

We may show ads from Google AdSense to keep the site free. When ads are shown:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics

We don’t currently use an analytics service. If we add one, we will update this policy before it goes live and name the provider here.

Children

The site is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
